The Problem

Mdantsane's economy is invisible online

🔴 What's broken

  • Businesses have no digital presence — customers can't find them
  • B2B sourcing relies on word-of-mouth WhatsApp chains
  • No way to verify trustworthiness before engaging
  • Foreign businesses take contracts that local providers could fill
  • Promising businesses stay small for lack of supply connections

🟢 What we built

  • Public directory — searchable, filterable, WhatsApp-first
  • B2B board — I Need / I Offer posts with instant contact
  • Trusted Trader — earned reputation badges (bronze → silver → gold)
  • Trade records — verified completion creates undeniable track record
  • Zero friction — no app install, works on any phone
